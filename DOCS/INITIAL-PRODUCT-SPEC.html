INITIAL PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
TCN IKORODU TRANSPORT
MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

A booking and management platform for church transport — so that every seat, every payment and every boarding is recorded, fair and traceable.

Draft for church review · Prepared by Saviour U. A. · October 2026

This is an Initial spec that requires revision for optimation opportunities
02 Executive summary — the meeting held · part 1 of 2

What was agreed.

A working session reviewed the whole system scope. These points were settled in the room — this specification is built on them.

Payments

Sunday-service seats stay free. Only designated special programmes charge, paid online securely. Refunds are automatic — but only for cancellations made before the event day.

Paying for a group

One member can book and pay for several people in a single payment. The payer appears on the receipt, and every rider is named — so each person has valid proof of their own seat.

How members receive it

An installable web app shared by one link — deliberately replacing an app-store listing. Members get push alerts plus email for confirmations, payment results and reminders.

Members only

Access is gated to congregation members — tied to the church register, not open signup. Signing in with Google proves identity; the register still decides membership.

Capacity per bus

A route's seats are the sum of its buses. The transport team can open or close booking per individual bus — one bus full doesn't close the route.

Booking windows automated

Bookings open and close on schedule automatically — with a manual override the team can still use.

Three screens, three roles

Separate dashboards confirmed for Members (book, pay, receipts), Marshals (boarding), and the Admin/transport team (buses, refunds, reports).

Absences counted

No-shows become a recorded status — visible for the first time, so attendance can finally be learned from.

03 Executive summary — the meeting held · part 2 of 2

What was scoped, and what's still open.

The meeting also mapped everything the platform must cover beyond booking — and honestly flagged what remains undecided.

Security & privacy

Protection beyond passwords — the app is the only door to member data; card details are never stored by the church, handled entirely by the payment provider.

A permanent record

Every booking, payment and admin change logged and attributed — and a review path so leaders can look back.

Fairness & safety limits

Sensible per-person usage limits so no single account — real or compromised — can block out seats or flood the system.

Backup & recovery

Automatic backups, a tested restore plan, and a clear difference between recovering a crash and responding to a breach.

Receipts & records storage

Receipts generated on demand from the live record — always accurate, nothing to lose. Whether proof-of-payment uploads are still needed is being decided.

Built for what comes next

The foundation is being laid so future church products can share it — reuse membership and records rather than rebuild.

Still to settle: the build technology, exactly how a seat is protected while someone pays, and how returning members who registered before are handled. These are technical — nothing on the following pages depends on the answer.

04 Why we're building this

Today, one person's phone is the whole system.

Seats promised in chat

Booking happens over WhatsApp and calls. Nobody knows a bus is full until people are standing beside it.

Payments by hand

Money goes to an admin who records names manually — whether a paid seat is actually confirmed lives in one person's memory.

Paper at the bus stop

Marshals tick names off printed lists. Absences are never recorded, so nothing can be learned or improved.

Nothing survives the week

No booking history means refund questions, capacity planning and reports all end in "we can't tell."

Confirmed with the Transport Department — this is the documented reality today
05 What it is

One journey, end to end.

A member opens a link, signs in, picks a programme, chooses a route and bus, sees exactly how many seats remain, books — and pays only when the programme calls for it. A marshal checks them aboard. Everything in between is recorded automatically.

The promise: capacity, payment and boarding become provable facts — not someone's recollection.

member → sign in → programme → route & pickup → seat held → payment → confirmed → boarded

and everything written to a permanent record
06 Who it serves

Built around four kinds of people.

The Member

Books a seat, pays where needed, carries a scannable ticket. Can also book and pay for family or friends in one payment.

The Marshal

Runs boarding at the pickup point on their phone — live passenger list, one-tap check-in. Assigned by the transport team.

The Transport Team

Opens and closes bookings per bus, manages refunds, sees live reports. Replaces paper, calls and memory.

Church Leadership

Sees the reports that matter — attendance, utilization, finances — and approves major policy decisions.

09 Paying for seats

Simple rules everyone can trust.

Sundays are free

Regular Sunday-service transport stays free — book and you're confirmed instantly.

Specials are paid securely

Online payment through a trusted Nigerian payment provider (bank card, transfer, USSD). Your seat is held while you complete payment.

Cash still works

We heard the transport department: some members will always pay in person. A manual-payment option stays available — recorded in the same system, on the same manifest.

You always know where you stand

Confirmed, pending or cancelled — your booking status is visible and notified, never assumed.

10 Changing your mind

Fair rules, applied the same for everyone.

Cancel before event dayAutomatic refund to your payment method — no calls needed
Cancel after the cutoffYour seat is released to someone waitlisted; payment retained — same as today's norm
Simply not showing upRecorded as a no-show — non-refundable

These rules match what the transport department already practices — the difference is they're now applied consistently, and recorded.

11 Day of travel

Boarding, minus the paper.

Live manifest

The marshal's phone shows the real passenger list for their bus — names, seats, group bookings — updated the moment bookings change.

One-tap check-in

Boarding is a tap per passenger. Absences are marked as no-shows — finally visible for the first time.

Made for the car park

Large touch targets, high contrast, works at 6 AM in the sun — and on older phones.

Paper as backup

A printable manifest stays available for device failures and offline moments.

13 Staying informed

The system tells you. You don't chase it.

Install once

The app installs from a link — no app store, no downloads folder, no fuss. It sits on the home screen like any app.

Booking confirmations

The moment a seat is yours — push notification and email with your reference.

Departure reminders

Alerts before your bus leaves, and if your booking status ever changes.

Works where members are

Designed for the phones and habits our congregation actually has — not the ones we wish they had.

14 Trust & accountability

A record that can't be quietly rewritten.

Every action is attributed

Who booked, who paid, who checked in, who changed what — permanently logged.

Corrections, not erasures

When something is wrong, a correction is added — pointing at the original. Nothing disappears.

Members only

Access is tied to the church register — the system knows who belongs.

Your data, respected

Only what's needed for transport is collected; reporting uses the minimum necessary.

Principle we build by: the system cannot be edited in place — so it can always be trusted
