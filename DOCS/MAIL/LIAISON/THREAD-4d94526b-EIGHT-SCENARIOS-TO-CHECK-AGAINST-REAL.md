We have written down the situations the system must handle: single trips, round trips, groups, guests, full buses, buses that do not come, cancellations, payments, and special events. The full list is in PRODUCT/SCENARIOS-AND-EDGE-CASES.md (125 rows).

Please check these against how the department works in practice. Short answers are fine.

1. Scenario E02: a bus does not come at all. What do the people on it expect today: a rebooking, a refund, or nothing?
2. Scenario E01: a bus is 40 minutes late. How long does it usually wait?
3. Scenario D11: a booked rider does not turn up. Is the seat usually paid for, and who decides?
4. Scenario A04: a round-trip rider wants the outbound bus, but the return bus is full. Can that rider book only the outbound leg today?
5. Scenario B13: a route has three buses and one is full. Do people normally move to another bus on the same route?
6. Scenario F04: a first-timer applies on Friday night for Sunday, and the usual administrator is away. Who decides?
7. Scenario E17: are children ever carried without a parent or guardian? If so, what is done today?
8. Scenario C12: when a member cannot pay online, what happens today? We are least sure about this case.

Thank you.
