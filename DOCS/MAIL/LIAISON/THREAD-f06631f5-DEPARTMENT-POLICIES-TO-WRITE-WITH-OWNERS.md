Thank you for the operational truths you sent on 7 October. We have now drafted the rules the department needs to write before the system can run. The list has 29 policies, with the scenarios that need each one. It is in GOVERNANCE/DEPARTMENT-POLICY-REGISTER.md, section 2.

Needed before the pilot:
- P-01 Refund rules: who is eligible, how much is refunded, exceptions, and who approves them
- P-03 What happens when a bus does not come, breaks down, or runs late
- P-05 What to tell waiting riders when no bus can be found
- P-07 What counts as a no-show, and what happens after repeated no-shows
- P-09 Who may bring guests, and how invite links are shared
- P-12 Late payments, and members who cannot pay online
- P-14 What a marshal may and may not do on duty
- P-16 Children travelling without a parent or guardian
- P-22 The admin rota and the second admin for approvals
- P-23 Support response targets and the finance contact
- P-25 A programme cancelled on the day
- P-28 What staff do when the app is down (the printed manifest)

Please reply with:
1. The owner in the department for each of the policies above, and the date the first draft can be ready. The refund policy is for the department to draft, as decided.
2. The name of the finance contact for payment problems.
3. The finance period for keeping payment records.
4. The name of the second administrator who approves special events.

Decisions already taken, for you to check against your own view:
- Every payment is digital, through Paystack and/or OPay. There is no cash and no POS. You told us cash is essential for some members. We have recorded that as a risk for the pilot to review, rather than as a settled question.
- The rider list locks 30 minutes before departure. A rider added after that needs admin approval with a reason.
- Cancellations close 12 hours before departure by default. An admin can set a different time for a programme.
- Guests: no cap at launch. The office will monitor guest numbers per bus.
- Waiting list: first-time guests are offered seats first, then other riders. Each offer lasts 2 hours.
