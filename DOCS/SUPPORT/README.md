## What this folder is for

How members and marshals ask for help, how those requests are recorded and owned, and how they are closed. Two channels run side by side: the in-app report (a record) and WhatsApp or phone (immediate, travel day). The in-app report never replaces the phone on travel day.

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `SUPPORT-SYSTEM.md` | Channels, categories and owners, statuses, response targets (proposed), privacy, escalation |

## Governing rules

- Travel-day emergencies go to the phone or WhatsApp first. The app records them afterwards.
- Support staff never ask for card or bank details.
- Internal notes never reach the reporter.
