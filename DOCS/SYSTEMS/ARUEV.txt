Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Decision Integrity & Verification
Dependencies: Universal Intelligence Kernel (UIK), ADCOS, MNIF, DQS, QOF

1. Purpose

The Assumption, Risk, Uncertainty, Evidence & Validation Engine (ARUEV) is responsible for identifying, measuring, validating, and continuously monitoring the integrity of every decision.

Every problem contains five unavoidable components:

Assumptions

Risks

Uncertainties

Evidence

Validation

ARUEV converts these implicit components into explicit, measurable, and continuously verifiable objects.

It answers:

"What must be true for this decision to remain valid?"

2. Responsibilities

ARUEV is responsible for:

Assumption extraction

Assumption validation

Assumption monitoring

Risk identification

Risk quantification

Risk propagation

Uncertainty measurement

Evidence aggregation

Evidence strength analysis

Evidence contradiction analysis

Validation planning

Validation execution

Decision integrity scoring

3. ARUEV Lifecycle

Decision ↓ Extract Assumptions ↓ Identify Risks ↓ Identify Uncertainties ↓ Collect Evidence ↓ Generate Validation Plan ↓ Execute Validation ↓ Integrity Assessment ↓ Continuous Monitoring 

4. Core Model

Every decision owns:

Assumptions

Risks

Uncertainties

Evidence

Validation Tests

Integrity Score

If any of these change, the decision must be re-evaluated.

6. Integrity Formula

The integrity score is computed from five pillars:

Decision Integrity = Assumption Quality + Evidence Quality + Validation Success - Risk Exposure - Uncertainty Penalty 

The exact weighting is configurable through the Kernel.

8. Revalidation Triggers

A decision automatically enters the revalidation queue if:

New evidence is added.

Existing evidence is invalidated.

An assumption changes.

Risk exceeds threshold.

Confidence decreases.

Regulations change.

Market conditions change.

External dependencies fail.

Monitoring detects anomalies.

10. Design Principles

Every assumption is explicit.

Every assumption is testable.

Every risk has measurable exposure.

Every uncertainty is quantified where possible.

Every piece of evidence is traceable.

Contradictory evidence is retained until resolved.

Validation is continuous, not a one-time event.

Decision integrity is dynamic and recalculated whenever underlying conditions change.

11. Foundation Summary

ARUEV is the decision integrity engine of the Universal Intelligence Operating System. It transforms assumptions, risks, uncertainties, evidence, and validation into first-class, measurable entities with explicit relationships and continuous monitoring. By ensuring that every decision is supported, challenged, and revalidated throughout its lifecycle, ARUEV provides the structural assurance necessary for trustworthy, domain-agnostic intelligent systems.
