DQS-004 · Decision Quality System (DQS)

Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Decision Assurance & Quality Control
Dependencies: Universal Intelligence Kernel (UIK), ADCOS, MNIF

1. Purpose

The Decision Quality System (DQS) measures, validates, improves, and certifies the quality of every decision produced by the Universal Intelligence Operating System.

DQS does not make decisions.

It determines whether a decision is complete, internally consistent, sufficiently supported, adequately simulated, appropriately constrained, and ready for execution.

The objective is to minimize low-quality decisions regardless of domain.

2. Core Principles

Every decision is measurable.

Quality is multidimensional.

Every score must be explainable.

Every score must be reproducible.

Confidence is not quality.

Evidence quality is measured independently.

Decisions remain provisional until certified.

Quality continuously evolves as new information arrives.

3. Decision Quality Pipeline

Decision Produced ↓ Completeness Analysis ↓ Consistency Analysis ↓ Evidence Assessment ↓ Assumption Assessment ↓ Constraint Verification ↓ Risk Assessment ↓ Simulation Coverage ↓ Confidence Assessment ↓ Quality Score ↓ Certification ↓ Execution Approval

9. Decision Quality Formula

The overall quality score is calculated from weighted dimensions:

Overall Quality Score = Σ (Dimension Score × Dimension Weight) ─────────────────────────────────────── Σ Dimension Weights 

Individual engines may contribute scores, but DQS owns the final certified result.

11. Foundation Summary

The Decision Quality System provides an objective assurance layer for the Universal Intelligence Operating System. It evaluates every decision across standardized quality dimensions, validates evidence and assumptions, measures readiness, and certifies execution eligibility. By separating decision generation from decision evaluation, DQS establishes a measurable, auditable, and continuously improvable standard for high-quality decision making across any domain.
