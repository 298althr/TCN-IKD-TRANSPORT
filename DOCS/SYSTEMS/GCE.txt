1. Purpose

The Governance, Compliance & Ethics Engine (GCE) governs every action performed by the Universal Intelligence Operating System.

It determines:

Whether an action is allowed.

Whether it complies with applicable policies.

Whether required approvals exist.

Whether legal, ethical, financial, and operational constraints are satisfied.

Whether execution should continue, pause, or terminate.

Unlike the Execution Engine, which performs work, the Governance Engine authorizes, constrains, audits, and supervises work.

It answers:

"Should this action be permitted, under what conditions, and who is accountable?"

10. Design Principles

Governance is independent of execution.

Every action is policy-evaluated.

Compliance is continuously monitored.

Ethics are explicitly assessed.

Every approval is attributable.

Audit records are immutable.

Exceptions are controlled and time-bound.

Governance rules are configurable and version-controlled.

11. Foundation Summary

The Governance, Compliance & Ethics Engine provides the trust and control layer of the Universal Intelligence Operating System. It enforces organizational policies, regulatory obligations, ethical principles, and authorization rules before and during execution, ensuring that every decision and action remains accountable, compliant, auditable, and aligned with organizational governance objectives.
