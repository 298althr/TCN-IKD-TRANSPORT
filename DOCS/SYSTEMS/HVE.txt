1. Purpose

The Hypothesis & Validation Engine (HVE) transforms knowledge into testable hypotheses and objectively determines whether those hypotheses should be accepted, rejected, or revised.

Every recommendation, prediction, strategy, diagnosis, policy, or decision is fundamentally a hypothesis until validated.

HVE answers one question:

"What must be proven before this can be considered true?"

2. Responsibilities

HVE is responsible for:

Hypothesis generation

Alternative hypothesis generation

Hypothesis decomposition

Null hypothesis creation

Test planning

Validation execution

Statistical evaluation

Logical validation

Experimental validation

Comparative evaluation

Hypothesis ranking

Confidence estimation

3. Validation Philosophy

Nothing is accepted because:

AI said so

An expert said so

A paper said so

Historical success exists

Everything must survive structured validation.

4. Hypothesis Lifecycle

Knowledge ↓ Generate Hypotheses ↓ Generate Alternatives ↓ Generate Null Hypothesis ↓ Design Validation ↓ Run Tests ↓ Evaluate Results ↓ Compute Confidence ↓ Accept / Reject / Revise ↓ Publish

9. Acceptance Criteria

A hypothesis is eligible for downstream recommendation only if:

Required validation tests are complete.

Evidence exceeds the configured threshold.

Logical consistency passes.

No unresolved critical contradictions exist.

Minimum robustness score is achieved.

Required approval gates are satisfied.

Otherwise, it is either revised or rejected.

11. Design Principles

Every hypothesis must be falsifiable.

Alternative explanations are mandatory.

Null hypotheses are explicitly modeled.

Validation methods are reusable.

Experimental evidence outweighs assumptions.

Every conclusion is reproducible.

Validation history is immutable.

Confidence is earned through evidence rather than assigned arbitrarily.

12. Foundation Summary

The Hypothesis & Validation Engine provides the scientific reasoning layer of the Universal Intelligence Operating System. It converts structured knowledge into testable hypotheses, evaluates them through multiple validation methods, compares competing explanations, and promotes only those that satisfy objective acceptance criteria. This creates a rigorous, domain-agnostic framework for transforming knowledge into validated decision candidates.
