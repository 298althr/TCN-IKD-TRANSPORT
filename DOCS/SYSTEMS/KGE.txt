KGE-007 · Knowledge Graph & Knowledge Engine (KGE)

Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Knowledge Construction & Reasoning
Dependencies: Universal Intelligence Kernel (UIK), ADCOS, MNIF, QOF, ARUEV

1. Purpose

The Knowledge Graph & Knowledge Engine (KGE) transforms validated observations, evidence, and research into structured knowledge.

Raw information is not knowledge.

Knowledge exists only after information has been:

Organized

Related

Validated

Contextualized

Versioned

Traceable

The Knowledge Engine answers:

"What do we know, why do we know it, how strongly do we know it, and how is it connected?"

Unlike the Memory Engine (which stores experience), KGE stores verified relationships between concepts.

2. Responsibilities

The Knowledge Engine is responsible for:

Knowledge construction

Ontology mapping

Concept extraction

Entity extraction

Relationship generation

Knowledge graph construction

Knowledge validation

Knowledge versioning

Knowledge querying

Contradiction detection

Semantic reasoning

Knowledge confidence

3. Knowledge Lifecycle

Observations ↓ Evidence ↓ Facts ↓ Concepts ↓ Relationships ↓ Knowledge Objects ↓ Knowledge Graph ↓ Reasoning ↓ Recommendations ↓ Learning 

4. Core Knowledge Model

Knowledge is composed of five components:

Concepts

Facts

Relationships

Context

Confidence

Knowledge is never stored as isolated documents. It is stored as interconnected objects.

11. Design Principles

Knowledge must be evidence-backed.

Every fact is traceable to its sources.

Relationships are first-class objects.

Contradictions are preserved until resolved.

Knowledge evolves through versioning.

Semantic relationships enable domain-independent reasoning.

Knowledge is reusable across workflows and domains.

Every inference is explainable through its reasoning path.

12. Foundation Summary

The Knowledge Graph & Knowledge Engine provides the semantic foundation of the Universal Intelligence Operating System. By transforming validated observations into interconnected concepts, facts, and relationships, it enables explainable reasoning, reusable intelligence, and domain-agnostic knowledge representation. It serves as the primary source of structured knowledge for all downstream reasoning and decision-making engines.
