MOE-013 · Monitoring & Observation Engine (MOE)

Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Operational Intelligence & Continuous Monitoring
Dependencies: UIK, ADCOS, EXE, QOF, DQS

1. Purpose

The Monitoring & Observation Engine (MOE) continuously measures reality after execution.

Previous engines predict what should happen.

The Monitoring Engine measures what actually happened.

It compares expected outcomes against actual outcomes, detects deviations, generates alerts, identifies emerging risks, and initiates corrective actions.

It answers:

"Is reality behaving as expected?"

10. Design Principles

Monitoring is continuous rather than periodic.

Expected and actual outcomes are measured independently.

Drift is tracked separately from anomalies.

Every alert is traceable to its triggering measurements.

Corrective actions are explicit objects.

Historical measurements are immutable.

Monitoring supports any measurable domain.

Operational feedback continuously improves future decisions.

11. Foundation Summary

The Monitoring & Observation Engine closes the execution loop by continuously comparing predicted outcomes with observed reality. Through real-time measurements, anomaly detection, trend analysis, and corrective action management, it provides the operational feedback required for adaptive, evidence-driven decision systems. MOE forms the primary bridge between execution and organizational learning.
