PRE-008 · Problem & Research Engine (PRE)

Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Problem Discovery & Knowledge Acquisition
Dependencies: Universal Intelligence Kernel (UIK), ADCOS, MNIF, QOF, KGE

1. Purpose

The Problem & Research Engine (PRE) is responsible for transforming ambiguous user requests into well-defined, research-backed problem statements.

Its philosophy is:

No solution should exist before the problem is sufficiently understood.

Every workflow entering the Universal Intelligence Operating System begins here unless an already-defined problem exists.

PRE answers four questions:

What is the real problem?

What information is missing?

What research is required?

When is the problem sufficiently understood?

2. Responsibilities

PRE is responsible for:

Problem discovery

Problem decomposition

Context acquisition

Research planning

Research execution

Evidence collection

Information gap analysis

Stakeholder identification

Requirement elicitation

Problem validation

Problem versioning

Research traceability

3. Problem Lifecycle

Initial Request ↓ Intent Detection ↓ Problem Identification ↓ Context Collection ↓ Research Planning ↓ Evidence Collection ↓ Gap Analysis ↓ Problem Refinement ↓ Problem Validation ↓ Knowledge Engine 

4. Core Principles

Separate symptoms from root problems.

Unknowns are first-class objects.

Every claim requires supporting evidence.

Research is iterative.

The problem definition evolves over time.

Multiple competing problem statements may coexist until validated.

6. Problem Decomposition Model

Every problem is decomposed into:

Problem ├── Objectives ├── Context ├── Constraints ├── Requirements ├── Stakeholders ├── Information Gaps ├── Root Causes ├── Research ├── Findings └── Readiness 

7. Research Workflow

Problem ↓ Generate Research Questions ↓ Identify Sources ↓ Collect Information ↓ Validate Information ↓ Generate Findings ↓ Identify Gaps ↓ Refine Problem ↓ Publish Knowledge 

8. Problem Readiness Criteria

A problem is considered research-ready only if:

Objectives are defined.

Scope is established.

Stakeholders are identified.

Required context is collected.

Major information gaps are documented.

Initial evidence exists.

Constraints are understood.

A problem is considered solution-ready only if the readiness assessment exceeds the configured threshold.

10. Design Principles

Problems are living objects that evolve.

Research is linked directly to the problem it supports.

Every finding is traceable to its source.

Information gaps are explicitly modeled.

Root causes are distinguished from symptoms.

Multiple hypotheses may exist simultaneously.

Research completion is measurable.

Problems are only promoted when predefined readiness criteria are met.

11. Foundation Summary

The Problem & Research Engine provides the discovery and investigation layer of the Universal Intelligence Operating System. It transforms ambiguous requests into structured, research-backed problem definitions by managing context, requirements, research activities, findings, information gaps, and readiness assessments. This ensures that downstream reasoning engines operate on well-defined problems supported by sufficient evidence rather than assumptions.
