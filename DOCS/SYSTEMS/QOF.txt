QOF-005 · Quality Observation Framework (QOF)

Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Observation, Measurement & Evidence Quality
Dependencies: Universal Intelligence Kernel (UIK), ADCOS, MNIF, DQS

1. Purpose

The Quality Observation Framework (QOF) is responsible for measuring the quality of observations before they become facts, knowledge, or evidence.

Every intelligent system ultimately depends on observations:

User statements

Market prices

Sensor readings

API responses

Documents

Images

Audio

Expert opinions

Research papers

Simulations

QOF determines:

"Can this observation be trusted, measured, reproduced, and used as evidence?"

Unlike DQS, which evaluates decisions, QOF evaluates inputs.

2. Responsibilities

QOF is responsible for:

Observation acquisition

Observation normalization

Observation scoring

Source reliability

Data quality measurement

Signal quality assessment

Noise detection

Bias detection

Missing information detection

Observation lineage

Observation reproducibility

Observation confidence

6. Observation Quality Formula

The overall observation quality is computed from weighted dimensions:

Observation Quality = Accuracy + Precision + Completeness + Consistency + Timeliness + Reliability ──────────────────────── Weighted Average 

Weights are configurable through the Kernel.

10. Design Principles

Every observation has provenance.

Every observation has measurable quality.

No observation is accepted without traceability.

Reliability is measured independently from confidence.

Contradictory observations are preserved rather than discarded.

Every transformation is recorded.

Evidence is derived from validated observations, not raw inputs.

Observation quality can be recalculated as standards evolve.

11. Foundation Summary

The Quality Observation Framework establishes the evidential foundation of the Universal Intelligence Operating System. By evaluating the quality, provenance, reliability, and reproducibility of every observation before it enters downstream intelligence pipelines, QOF ensures that higher-level reasoning engines operate on trusted, measurable, and auditable information rather than unverified data.
