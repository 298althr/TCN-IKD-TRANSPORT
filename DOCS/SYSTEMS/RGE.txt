RGE-011 · Recommendation & Generation Engine (RGE)

Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Decision Synthesis & Solution Generation
Dependencies: UIK, ADCOS, MNIF, DQS, ARUEV, KGE, HVE, SME

1. Purpose

The Recommendation & Generation Engine (RGE) synthesizes all validated intelligence into actionable recommendations.

Unlike previous engines that gather, validate, or simulate information, RGE is responsible for generating candidate solutions and ranking them according to objective quality metrics.

RGE answers:

"Given everything we know, what should be done, why, and what are the alternatives?"

RGE never invents unsupported recommendations. Every recommendation must be traceable to validated knowledge, simulations, evidence, constraints, and objectives.

10. Design Principles

Recommendations must be evidence-backed.

Multiple alternatives are mandatory.

Trade-offs are explicit.

Every recommendation is explainable.

Recommendations are ranked objectively.

Implementation planning is integrated into recommendation generation.

Success criteria are defined before execution.

Recommendations remain version-controlled and auditable.

11. Foundation Summary

The Recommendation & Generation Engine transforms validated knowledge into actionable, explainable, and prioritized recommendations. By synthesizing evidence, simulations, risks, constraints, and objectives into structured solution packages with implementation plans and measurable success criteria, RGE provides the actionable output layer of the Universal Intelligence Operating System while preserving traceability and decision integrity.
