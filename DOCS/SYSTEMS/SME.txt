Version: 1.0
Status: Core Intelligence Engine
Layer: Predictive Analysis & Scenario Exploration
Dependencies: UIK, ADCOS, MNIF, PRE, KGE, HVE, ARUEV

1. Purpose

The Simulation & Modeling Engine (SME) evaluates possible futures before execution.

Rather than predicting a single outcome, SME explores multiple scenarios, quantifies uncertainty, evaluates trade-offs, and estimates the consequences of different decisions.

SME answers:

"If this decision is executed, what are the most probable outcomes under different conditions?"

4. Core Concepts

Model

A mathematical or logical representation of reality.

Scenario

A specific set of assumptions and inputs.

Simulation

Execution of a model using one scenario.

Outcome

The measurable result of a simulation.

Digital Twin

A continuously synchronized virtual representation of a real system.

10. Design Principles

Multiple futures are evaluated simultaneously.

Every simulation is reproducible.

Models are version-controlled.

Parameters are explicitly recorded.

Scenario comparisons are first-class objects.

Uncertainty is quantified rather than ignored.

Optimization is evidence-driven.

Every simulation contributes to organizational learning.

11. Foundation Summary

The Simulation & Modeling Engine provides the predictive reasoning capability of the Universal Intelligence Operating System. By executing multiple models across diverse scenarios, quantifying uncertainty, and evaluating trade-offs before execution, SME enables informed, evidence-based decision making in a domain-agnostic manner while maintaining complete traceability and reproducibility.
